Arb density certification results
=================================

Command (python-flint 0.9.0):
  python race_density_arb.py

Conditional mathematical inputs:
  * GRH + LI for the Rubinstein--Sarnak limiting-variable model;
  * the zero localization/completeness certificates from certify_arb.py.

Numerical certification:
  * Arb precision: 192 bits;
  * listed zero ordinates widened by +/- 1e-20;
  * sigma^2 enclosed with Arb plus an explicit Cauchy remainder;
  * 16-point composite Gauss--Legendre nodes and weights isolated in Arb;
  * analytic Gauss remainder bounded by Cauchy's estimate;
  * unseen-tail model error bounded by adaptive interval subdivision;
  * infinite-t tail bounded using Landau's global J_0 envelope.

Every lower bound below was rounded downward and every upper bound upward.
The rounding is performed by Arb integer floor/ceiling and each printed
decimal is reparsed to verify outward containment.  The adaptive E1 integral
is summed afresh from its active leaf intervals; no subtractive interval
bookkeeping is used.  A post-repair audit by a second internal AI agent exercised both
mechanisms on adversarial examples before the full rerun below.
